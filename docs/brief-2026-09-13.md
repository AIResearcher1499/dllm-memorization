## 1. What B is

**Question.** How many bits per parameter does a masked diffusion LM (MDM) memorize, compared
with a matched autoregressive (AR) model, under the Morris et al. protocol (arXiv:2505.24832,
ICML 2026 oral: GPT-style AR ≈ 3.6 bits/param, generalization is *forced* once data exceeds
capacity)? Two quantities, kept separate by prereg: **capacity** (plateau height of total
unintended memorization as |D| grows) and **rate** (steps to plateau at fixed |D|).

**Why it matters.** MDMs' celebrated data-reuse advantage (arXiv:2507.15857: repeated data ≈
fresh up to ~100 epochs vs ~4 for AR) is unpriced if part of it is a memorization advantage;
and the field's AR-vs-MDM comparisons compare AR exact NLL against a raw MDM ELBO — a bias
nobody has quantified.

**Methodological core (the part ML reviewers recognize).** Morris capacity is
likelihood-based; MDMs give an ELBO, not an exact NLL. Resolution, from the 24/08 sweep:
1. RADD (arXiv:2406.03736): the absorbing-diffusion ELBO equals the expected code length of an
   any-order AR model → on uniform random data it is an *achievable* compression length, so
   ELBO-based memorized bits are a valid **lower bound** on capacity (EST-1).
2. Fixed left-to-right decoding + chain rule gives the **exact** NLL of the induced AR model
   (SMDM practice) (EST-2).
3. Likelihood-free cross-check: extraction rate — greedy prefix (Morris) and edge-conditioned
   infilling masks (arXiv:2605.24173, which extract up to 3× more from DLMs than prefix) (EST-3).
Agreement/disagreement among the three is itself a result the field needs.

**Registered confound.** arXiv:2510.04071 argues MDM data-reuse gains are largely
token-dropout-style input corruption reproducible in AR → B-1 carries an **AR + masked-input
augmentation** arm. (New since freeze: arXiv:2606.06888 gives an MIR recipe + scaling laws for
exactly this control — reuse it.)

**Registered expectations (hypotheses, not assumptions).** E1: MDM approaches its plateau
more slowly (only ~50% of positions trained per step). E2: direction of the capacity contrast
is *not* predicted — higher, lower, equal are all live findings. The only KILL is instrument
failure (estimator gap or seed noise larger than any plausible contrast).

## 2. What C is, and how it folds in

C ("tokenizer counterfactual → training-dynamics quantities") uses the unpublished **F0
method** (one corpus, one fixed vocab, dose-controlled tokenizers, monotone 1.5–2.1× fertility
dose per language) to attach tokenizer granularity to dynamics quantities. Re-check 2026-09-13:
arXiv:2609.01386 (*When Tokenization is Secretly Output Supervision*) now owns the generic
"tokenizer granularity shapes training dynamics and internals" mechanism (numeric reasoning,
controlled input/output decoupling); arXiv:2606.29858 (token-level learning-time spectrum) is
a usable instrument; arXiv:2605.01188 (compute-optimal tokenization: parameters scale with
**bytes**, not tokens) remains the landmine. Standalone, C drifts ACL-shaped.

**As a second axis of B it becomes a crisp general question:** *is memorization capacity,
measured in bits of text, invariant to tokenizer granularity?* Morris measured on a fixed
V=2048 synthetic vocab; 2605.01188's bytes-not-tokens result hints at invariance for loss
scaling but nobody has tested it for capacity. Either answer is a finding; the F0 tokenizers
and the B model zoo are reused. This axis is written as a dated amendment **only after B-0
lands with margin** — never bolted on before the gate.

## 5. Cost to run

Assumptions: cloud H100 at ≈ $2.5–3.5/h (spot/on-demand 2026); tiny models are kernel-launch
bound, so an H100 is only ≈ 1.5–2× an A6000 here; the 2×A6000 box costs ≈ $0 but serializes
wall-clock. FLOP check for the largest B-0 cell: 6 × 14.5M × (2048 × 64 tokens) ≈ 1.1e13
FLOP/step → 1e6 steps ≈ 1.1e19 FLOP ≈ 20 h on an H100 at ~150 TFLOP/s effective; most cells
plateau far below the cap.

## 7. What would stop this

- B-0 fails the α gate → estimator/pipeline bug; fix, re-run; cannot kill the idea.
- G0-B KILL (estimator gap or seed noise > any AR-vs-MDM contrast) → bury; do not retune.
- RATE-LIMITED (MDM curves still rising at the 2e6 cap) → registered separate outcome → step-cap
  amendment decision, not silent continuation.
- A same-claim preprint appears before ours → monthly arXiv watch (`recheck-dllm-memorization`
  lane, `fetch --days 30`).
